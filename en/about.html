Profile

Attorney Zainab Ali Madan

A Bahraini lawyer and legal consultant, and founder of the Lawyer Zainab Ali Madan Office for advocacy and legal consultancy in Manama. She provides professional legal services close to the client, combining precision in the work with clarity in communication.

She holds a Bachelor of Law from the University of Bahrain, has ten years of experience in legal practice, and is licensed to appear before the Court of Cassation — the highest level of litigation in the Kingdom of Bahrain. Alongside advocacy, she has an interest in governance and in helping organisations build sound regulatory frameworks.
