FAQ

Quick answers

Who must prove that the salary was paid?

Usually the burden falls on the employer. In our published case the company could not prove payment, and judgment was given for the employee.

Does the judgment include leave balance?

It may include cash in lieu of annual leave, as happened in one of our cases.

Can I claim while still employed?

Yes, in many situations; we advise on the best approach so your position is not affected.

Do you represent expatriate workers?

Yes. We represent employees of all nationalities and work in Arabic and English.
