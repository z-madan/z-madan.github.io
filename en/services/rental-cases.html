FAQ

Quick answers

Can a tenant be evicted immediately for late payment?

Eviction happens through the legal process and by court judgment, never by force. We identify the correct procedure for your case.

Can I claim rent and eviction together?

Often both claims can be combined; we confirm this after reviewing the lease.

What if there is no written lease?

The tenancy can be proven by other means such as transfers and correspondence.

Do you represent tenants too?

Yes. We represent landlords and tenants depending on the case.
